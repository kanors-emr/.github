# KanORS-EMR

KanORS builds modeling environments and ready-to-use energy system models that let researchers, businesses, and policymakers explore transition choices directly.

KanORS has been part of the TIMES framework since its creation, and is the sole developer of Veda, the standard environment for building and running TIMES models. The aim is to let non-programmers build models and non-modelers use them.

## Products

- **[Veda](https://kanors-emr.github.io/Veda-documentation/)** — data handling, scenario management, and results analysis for TIMES models. Use it on the desktop with Veda 2.0, or in the browser with [Veda Online](https://vedaonline.cloud/).
- **KiNESYS+** — bespoke, high-resolution models for policy and investment decisions.
- **[VerveStacks](https://vervestacks.cloud/)** — ready-to-run models for 190+ countries, built from open datasets and free to use.
